NASA SLI Payload Assembly

Role: Mechanical Design / CAD

Project Overview

Designed and developed CAD models of the avionics bay for both subscale and full-scale configurations in NASA’s Student Launch Initiative, ensuring compliance with strict mass, volumetric, and structural requirements.

Design Breakdown

  • Modeled fully constrained assemblies with realistic mates, ensuring proper measurements
  • Integrated a range of McMaster-Carr components to construct the bulkhead assembly and the individual electronic components
  • Considered manufacturability and fastener selection for real-world assembly
  • Iterated designs based on various forms of testing and team feedback
